## State and evidence definitions

Use the approved Learning State and E0–E5 definitions in [Knowledge & Evidence Governance](../core/knowledge-and-evidence-governance.md). This register stores current local values and evidence pointers; it does not define a parallel scale.

## Provenance requirements

Every assessed row must identify participant, task/context, local evidence artifact, exact local source reference, assessment method, assessor/reviewer, date, simulation/real-world status and limitations.

## Project boundary

Do not import, copy, summarize or reconstruct another project's competency claims or evidence in this register. Independent projects remain authoritative for their own learning history and competencies. A competency demonstrated elsewhere is not automatically recorded or promoted here.
